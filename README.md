# Consumer Preference Market Research: Automated Delivery Lockers

This repository contains a quantitative market research project analyzing consumer behavior and preferences regarding automated delivery lockers (EasyBox) in Romania. The project validates business assumptions through statistical hypothesis testing, providing actionable insights for strategic infrastructure investments.

## Project Overview & Methodology
The research evaluates the adoption rates, primary motivations, and demographic differences in customer satisfaction regarding SmartBox Lockers compared to traditional home delivery. 
*   **Data Collection:** Survey data was collected via Google Forms from a sample of 46 valid respondents using non-probability snowball sampling across social media platforms.
*   **Data Processing & Analysis:** Raw data was cleaned and pre-processed in Microsoft Excel, followed by rigorous statistical analysis and hypothesis testing using IBM SPSS Statistics.
*   **Demographics:** The analyzed sample consisted of approximately 60% female and 40% male respondents, with 87% residing in urban environments and a majority holding a university degree.

## Key Business Insights & Statistical Findings
*   **High Adoption Rate:** A significant majority of 73.9% of respondents prefer SmartBox Lockers over traditional home delivery. Furthermore, over 80% of users utilize the service frequently, with 47.8% selecting it every few orders and 34.8% using it for every order.
*   **Primary Driver (Binomial Test):** "Flexible scheduling" is the dominant motivation, selected by 84.8% of respondents. A Binomial test statistically confirmed ($p < 0.001$) that this flexibility is the absolute primary driver for consumers choosing this delivery method, significantly exceeding the 60% test proportion threshold.
*   **Secondary Motivations:** Other notable reasons for choosing automated lockers include reduced costs (43.5%), faster pick-up times (39.1%), and proximity to home (30.4%).
*   **Urban vs. Rural Cost Perception (Independent Samples T-Test):** An Independent Samples T-test revealed a statistically significant difference ($p = 0.021$, $t = 2.387$) in how urban and rural demographics perceive delivery costs. Urban residents reported a higher cost satisfaction mean (3.80 out of 5) compared to rural residents (2.67 out of 5), likely due to lower locker density and reduced accessibility in rural areas.
*   **Age and Usage Frequency (Chi-Square Test):** A Pearson Chi-Square test ($p = 0.722$) indicated no statistically significant relationship between a consumer's age and their frequency of using the locker service, demonstrating that the service appeals broadly across different age demographics.

## Strategic Recommendations
Based on the statistical analysis, the following strategic business actions are recommended:
*   **Rural Expansion:** Invest in expanding the locker network infrastructure in rural areas to improve accessibility and elevate the currently lower cost satisfaction among rural consumers.
*   **Marketing Focus:** Center commercial communication strategies around the autonomy and flexible schedule provided by the lockers, as this is proven to be the most valued competitive advantage.
*   **Pricing Strategy:** Maintain transparent and competitive pricing to consolidate the positive perception in urban areas while mitigating the cost-related dissatisfaction identified in rural regions.
