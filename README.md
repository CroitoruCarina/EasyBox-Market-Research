# Consumer Preference Market Research: Automated Delivery Lockers

This repository contains a quantitative market research project analyzing consumer behavior and preferences regarding automated delivery lockers (EasyBox) in Romania[cite: 2]. The project validates business assumptions through statistical hypothesis testing, providing actionable insights for strategic infrastructure investments[cite: 2].

## Project Overview & Methodology
The research evaluates the adoption rates, primary motivations, and demographic differences in customer satisfaction regarding SmartBox Lockers compared to traditional home delivery[cite: 2]. 
*   **Data Collection:** Survey data was collected via Google Forms from a sample of 46 valid respondents using non-probability snowball sampling across social media platforms[cite: 2].
*   **Data Processing & Analysis:** Raw data was cleaned and pre-processed in Microsoft Excel, followed by rigorous statistical analysis and hypothesis testing using IBM SPSS Statistics[cite: 2].
*   **Demographics:** The analyzed sample consisted of approximately 60% female and 40% male respondents, with 87% residing in urban environments and a majority holding a university degree[cite: 2].

## Key Business Insights & Statistical Findings
*   **High Adoption Rate:** A significant majority of 73.9% of respondents prefer SmartBox Lockers over traditional home delivery[cite: 2]. Furthermore, over 80% of users utilize the service frequently, with 47.8% selecting it every few orders and 34.8% using it for every order[cite: 2].
*   **Primary Driver (Binomial Test):** "Flexible scheduling" is the dominant motivation, selected by 84.8% of respondents[cite: 2]. A Binomial test statistically confirmed ($p < 0.001$) that this flexibility is the absolute primary driver for consumers choosing this delivery method, significantly exceeding the 60% test proportion threshold[cite: 2].
*   **Secondary Motivations:** Other notable reasons for choosing automated lockers include reduced costs (43.5%), faster pick-up times (39.1%), and proximity to home (30.4%)[cite: 2].
*   **Urban vs. Rural Cost Perception (Independent Samples T-Test):** An Independent Samples T-test revealed a statistically significant difference ($p = 0.021$, $t = 2.387$) in how urban and rural demographics perceive delivery costs[cite: 2]. Urban residents reported a higher cost satisfaction mean (3.80 out of 5) compared to rural residents (2.67 out of 5), likely due to lower locker density and reduced accessibility in rural areas[cite: 2].
*   **Age and Usage Frequency (Chi-Square Test):** A Pearson Chi-Square test ($p = 0.722$) indicated no statistically significant relationship between a consumer's age and their frequency of using the locker service, demonstrating that the service appeals broadly across different age demographics[cite: 2].

## Strategic Recommendations
Based on the statistical analysis, the following strategic business actions are recommended:
*   **Rural Expansion:** Invest in expanding the locker network infrastructure in rural areas to improve accessibility and elevate the currently lower cost satisfaction among rural consumers[cite: 2].
*   **Marketing Focus:** Center commercial communication strategies around the autonomy and flexible schedule provided by the lockers, as this is proven to be the most valued competitive advantage[cite: 2].
*   **Pricing Strategy:** Maintain transparent and competitive pricing to consolidate the positive perception in urban areas while mitigating the cost-related dissatisfaction identified in rural regions[cite: 2].
